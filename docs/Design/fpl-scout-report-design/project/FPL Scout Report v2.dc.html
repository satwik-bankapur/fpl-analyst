Gordon's minutes have quietly become conditional — two of the last four starts came in games already decided, and he was the first man off in both. Mbeumo is the opposite: nailed on the left of a front three, taking the corners, and playing three of the softest fixtures in the division before the international break. It costs £0.8m, which you have. Do it Friday, not now — Gordon may yet be flagged, and a flag makes this decision for you.

Captaincy

Palmer, but hold your nerve on Sunday. He is on penalties, on free kicks, and playing a side that has conceded from open play in nine straight games. The ownership makes it feel obvious rather than smart, but obvious is correct this week — you are chasing a rank, not a story. If he's benched, move the armband to Isak and take the small loss.

Bench order & the plan

Your bench order is costing about 4 points a week. Put Kluivert first — the only bench player with a realistic path to a return, and he plays Saturday lunchtime, so you learn something before the rest of your team kicks off. The fortnight plan is deliberately dull: roll the transfer in GW12 if nothing breaks, then two moves in GW13 to fix the defence when the swing arrives. Write that down now, because GW12 will tempt you into something worse.

Chips

None of these is worth a hit; all three are worth ten minutes on Saturday before you commit a transfer next week.

05 / Palette & type scale Tailwind-ready · dark default, light variant included
