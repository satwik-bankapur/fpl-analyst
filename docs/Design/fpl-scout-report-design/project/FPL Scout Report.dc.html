Your bench order is costing about 4 points a week.

Put Kluivert first. He is the only bench player with a realistic path to a return, and he plays Saturday lunchtime, which means you learn something before the rest of your team kicks off. Your current first sub is a £4.0m defender in a side that has kept one clean sheet since August.

The plan for the next fortnight is deliberately dull: roll the transfer in GW12 if nothing breaks, then use two moves in GW13 to fix the defence properly when the fixture swing arrives. Write that down now, because GW12 will tempt you into something worse.

05 · Chips

Don't play the Wildcard this week.

Your squad is three players away from where you want it, and a Wildcard spent to fix three players is a Wildcard wasted. The window that matters is GW13 to GW15, when the fixtures turn and half the top-owned defence has a blank.

Bench Boost is dead until your bench is worth boosting — see the section above. Keep the Triple Captain for a double gameweek; you have plenty of season left and no reason to spend it on a home fixture you already expect to win.

06 · Watchlist

Three names to hold in your head, not your team.

Hall at £4.9m is the cheapest route into a back line that is about to get three winnable games, and he is taking attacking touches no £4.9m defender should be near. Semenyo is one goal away from a price rise and a very crowded bandwagon. And there is a £4.4m midfielder starting in a front three from Saturday who nobody has noticed yet — Ferguson, and only if the manager confirms it Friday.

None of these is worth a hit. All three are worth watching for ten minutes on Saturday before you commit a transfer next week.
